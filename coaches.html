DR. SHANNON MARTINEZ, PhD, BCBA-D

USA Boxing Certified Bronze Level Coach • Board Certified Behavior Analyst (Doctoral)

Boxing Behavior Coaching is different from classic boxing training models because it is data-based, goal-driven, and individualized to each person. It uses behavior science and direct assessment to identify skills, deficits, and personalized strategies for each athlete.

BOUYA Behavior Solutions LLC was created in 2020 by Dr. Shannon Martinez, PhD, BCBA-D to help people take control of their health, fitness, and daily habits. Located in Greenville, SC, Dr. Shannon Martinez brings years of experience providing systematic behavior change strategies with women and kids of all abilities.

As a USA Boxing Certified Bronze Level Coach and doctoral-level Behavior Analyst, Dr. Shannon Martinez specializes in helping women overcome gym burnout through boxing skills and behavioral principles, as well as empowering kids to build boxing skills, endurance, coordination, and athletic confidence.

When you work with Dr. Shannon Martinez, you will get 1:1 boxing training, identify your boxing, fitness and wellness goals and barriers, build individualized Boxing Conditioning routines, identify strategies that work for you, and build boxing skills and accountability so that you can make gains!

Behavior science includes systematic methodology that is foundational to behavior change so that clients can meet individualized boxing and wellness goals! Several coaching packages are available to fit your schedule and ambitions.
