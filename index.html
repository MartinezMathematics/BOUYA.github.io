WHAT IS BOXING BEHAVIOR COACHING?

Created in 2020 by Dr. Shannon Martinez, PhD, BCBA-D, a USA Boxing Certified Bronze Level Coach located in Greenville, SC. Boxing Behavior Coaching applies a rigorous scientific process to solve fitness, endurance, and lifestyle challenges—never guessing or relying on pseudo science. We build fun, individualized, boxing-style programs with accountability and skill development.

Neuromotor Conditioning

High-tempo combination drills and footwork calibrate hand-eye coordination, rapid somatic decisions, and motor impulse control.

Stress Inoculation & ABA

Applied Behavior Analysis protocols systematically teach emotional composure and diaphragmatic recovery when the heart rate peaks.

Measurable Goal Data

Progress is tracked through observable, objective behavioral metrics and structured reinforcement schedules—never guesswork.
