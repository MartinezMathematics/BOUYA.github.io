ABOUT BOUYA

BOUYA Behavior Solutions LLC was created in 2020 by Dr. Shannon Martinez, PhD, BCBA-D, a USA Boxing Certified Bronze Level Coach located in Greenville, SC, to help people take control of their health, fitness, and daily habits.

ESTABLISHED 2020

DR. SHANNON MARTINEZ, PhD, BCBA-D

USA Boxing Certified Bronze Level Coach • Board Certified Behavior Analyst (Doctoral)

“BOUYA Behavior Solutions LLC was created in 2020 by Dr. Shannon Martinez, PhD, BCBA-D to help people take control of their emotional regulation, daily habits, and somatic discipline.”

For years, mental performance and physical athletic training operated in isolated silos. Athletes worked hard in the gym without structured behavioral habit science, while clients struggled to translate good intentions into consistent, sustainable habits.

Dr. Martinez created Boxing Behavior Coaching to bridge this gap. Rather than relying on pseudo science, hearsay, or guesswork, Dr. Martinez applies a systematic scientific process: isolating barriers, creating individualized boxing-style fitness plans, and suggesting small, high-leverage changes that make a profound difference in life, skill development, fitness journeys, and boxing performance.

Whether helping mature women (ages 30–50s) overcome sluggishness, work-life imbalance, and impersonal gym burnout, or empowering young girls (ages 5–17) with coordination, endurance, and focus, every coaching program is grounded in authentic pugilistic technique and evidence-based behavioral principles.

PRIVATE TRAINING STUDIO

Located in Greenville, SC, BOUYA is designed specifically for focused, distraction-free 1-on-1 and small group behavioral boxing sessions for mature women (ages 30+) and children (ages 5–17).

Our studio pairs heavy bag rows, speed bags, mitt work stations, and agility lines with empirical behavioral assessment tools to identify skills, deficits, and personalized strategies for each athlete.
