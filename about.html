ESTABLISHED 2020

DR. SHANNON MARTINEZ, PhD, BCBA-D

USA Boxing Certified Bronze Level Coach • Board Certified Behavior Analyst (Doctoral)

BOUYA Behavior Solutions LLC was created in 2020 by Dr. Shannon Martinez, PhD, BCBA-D to help people take control of their emotional regulation, daily habits, and somatic discipline.

For years, mental performance and physical athletic training operated in isolated silos. Athletes worked hard in the gym without structured behavioral habit science, while clients struggled to translate good intentions into consistent, sustainable habits.

Dr. Shannon Martinez created Boxing Behavior Coaching to bridge this gap. Rather than relying on pseudo science, hearsay, or guesswork, Dr. Shannon Martinez applies a systematic scientific process: isolating barriers, creating individualized boxing-style fitness plans, and suggesting small, high-leverage changes that make a profound difference in life, skill development, fitness journeys, and boxing performance.

Whether helping women overcome sluggishness, work-life imbalance, and impersonal gym burnout, or empowering kids with coordination, endurance, and focus, every coaching program is grounded in authentic pugilistic technique and evidence-based behavioral principles.

EVOLVE BOXING & FITNESS CLUB

BOUYA operates out of Evolve Boxing and Fitness Club in Greenville, SC, providing focused 1-on-1 and small group behavioral boxing sessions for women and kids.

Training at Evolve Boxing and Fitness Club pairs heavy bag rows, speed bags, mitt work stations, and agility lines with empirical behavioral assessment tools to identify skills, deficits, and personalized strategies for each athlete.
